Privacy

Application privacy

Your application details and optional PDF CV are sent directly to Markforth when you click Submit application. They are stored privately on our hosting for recruitment review. Saving a draft in this browser tab is optional; CV files and consent choices are not included in the draft.

The team uses your information to review your application and contact you about recruitment. You can track published recruitment updates with your reference and an email verification code. Codes expire after 10 minutes; verified tracking access expires after 30 minutes. HR can send reviewed status messages to your application email. A session cookie protects the submission process. Contact info@markforthsol.com with questions about your application or information.
